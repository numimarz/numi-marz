ROADMAP // WHERE MARZ IS GOING

Software first. Hardware next.

Near-term work centers on growing Buddy, expanding its Studio and integrations, strengthening infrastructure for larger user demand, developing Alien by Marz as the consumer-commerce arm, and preparing Marz Mobile for future launch research.

Longer-term plans include Buddy voice and speaker connectivity, dedicated Marz devices, a Marz phone, persistent AI identity and memory research, multimodal AI, robotics and embodied intelligent systems through Marz AI Labs.
